Welcome

The meadows of Azaiba.

Al Murooj means “the meadows”, and that is the kind of place this is: a calm, residential corner of Muscat where the streets are wide, the neighbours are families and small offices, and the sea is a twenty-minute walk away. The hotel has been welcoming guests here since 2015.

Inside, there is more space than a hotel room gives you. Every apartment has a living room to spread out in, a kitchen to cook in and a bedroom to close the door on. Standard rooms keep it simple for shorter stays. A lift, a 24-hour front desk and a car park that is always free tie it together.

Business travellers come for the location: Azaiba and neighbouring Ghubrah hold many of Muscat's offices, and the airport is a straight ten-minute run down Sultan Qaboos Street. Families come for the kitchens, the quiet, and a price that leaves room in the budget for Oman itself.

Services

Small things, done every day.

Airport transfers

Pick-up or drop-off at Muscat International Airport, ten minutes away, for a small charge. Send us your flight number and we'll be waiting.

Car hire

Rent a car through the front desk and keep it in our free car park. Oman is a driving country; the mountains and wadis start an hour away.

Laundry & dry cleaning

Hand it in at reception and have it back the next day. Useful on a long stay, essential after a day in the wadis.

24-hour front desk

Late flight? Early flight? Someone is always at the desk, the lift runs all night, and check-in at 4 am is no trouble.

Luggage & wake-up calls

Leave your bags with us before check-in or after check-out, free of charge. Ask for a wake-up call and we'll ring on time.

Safe and sound

Keycard entry, CCTV in public areas, smoke detectors and fire alarms on every floor, and a safe deposit box at reception.

Where we are

Azaiba North, between the airport and the city.

Al Murooj sits on a quiet street in Azaiba North, one block off Sultan Qaboos Street, Muscat's main artery. Turn west for the airport, east for the malls, the Opera House and old Mutrah.

Getting here

  1. From the airport: a taxi takes 10 to 15 minutes along Sultan Qaboos Street. Or send us your flight number on WhatsApp and we'll arrange a pick-up.
  2. By bus: Mwasalat city buses run along Sultan Qaboos Street and stop at Azaiba, a five-minute walk from the door.
  3. By car: leave Sultan Qaboos Street at the Azaiba exit, head towards the sea and we are one block in. Free parking at the hotel.

Explore

Muscat, from your doorstep.

The capital stretches along fifty kilometres of coast between the mountains and the sea. From Azaiba, everything on this list is within half an hour.

8 min by car

Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque

Marble courtyards, a 14-tonne chandelier and one of the largest hand-woven carpets in the world. Open to visitors Saturday to Thursday mornings.

25 min by car

Mutrah Souq

Frankincense, silver, lanterns and pashminas under the old souq's wooden roofs. Go after five, when the alleys fill up.

25 min by car

Mutrah Corniche

A sunset walk along the harbour, from the fish market to the fort, with the dhows and the Sultan's yacht at anchor.

30 min by car

Al Alam Palace & Old Muscat

The Sultan's ceremonial palace, flanked by the Portuguese forts of Al Jalali and Al Mirani and the National Museum.

12 min by car

Royal Opera House Muscat

Opera, ballet and Arabic music in a hall of marble and teak. Tours by day, performances by night, and a galleria of cafés.

15 min by car

Qurum Beach

Muscat's long city beach: joggers at dawn, families at dusk, and a row of cafés along the shore road.

15 min by car

Al Mouj Marina

Waterfront restaurants, a marina walk and a golf course by The Wave, on the way to the airport.

20 min on foot

Azaiba Beach

The neighbourhood's own stretch of sand, quiet enough for a morning walk and an evening swim.

How far is the hotel from Muscat airport?

About 10 km, a 10 to 15 minute drive along Sultan Qaboos Street. Taxis wait outside arrivals; we can also send a car if you share your flight number on WhatsApp.

Do the apartments have kitchens?

Yes. Every one- and two-bedroom apartment has a kitchen with a gas hob, microwave, fridge and kettle. Tell us if you plan to cook so the basic cookware is in place when you arrive. Standard rooms have a fridge and kettle.

Is breakfast included?

No. A continental breakfast is OMR 2 per person; let reception know the evening before.

Is parking free?

Yes. Private parking at the hotel is free for guests, for as long as you stay.

Can I pay by card?

Yes. Visa, Mastercard and American Express are accepted, as is cash in Omani rials. Direct bookings need no prepayment.

Do you offer weekly or monthly rates?

Yes. Weekly and monthly rates are well below the nightly rate. Send your dates and the apartment type on WhatsApp and we'll quote the same day.

Is there a lift?

Yes, to every floor, and the main entrance is step-free.

What is the cancellation policy?

For direct bookings, tell us on WhatsApp as early as you can. We confirm the terms with your quote, and there is nothing to pay until you arrive.

Ready when you are.

Send your dates on WhatsApp and we'll reply with availability and a price. No prepayment, no booking fee, no middleman.
